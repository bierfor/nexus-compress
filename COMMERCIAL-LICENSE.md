## Why a Commercial License?

The AGPL-3.0 is a strong copyleft license. Section 13 ("Remote Network
Interaction") requires that **any modified version that users interact with
over a network must provide the source code of those modifications to those
users**. This is the right thing for community OSS, but it creates friction
for proprietary SaaS products that embed NexusCompress as a compression or
P2P-transfer component.

If your company wants to:

- Embed NexusCompress in a **proprietary product** (desktop, mobile, server)
- Run NexusCompress as part of a **hosted service** without releasing your
  internal modifications
- Use NexusCompress in **regulated environments** where copyleft is incompatible
  with the deployment model

…then you need a Commercial License.

1. Email **licensing@nexuscompress.dev** with:
